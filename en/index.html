FAQ

Before you ask.

I already have Instagram. Do I really need a website?

Instagram shows; a website explains and convinces. It answers “what you do, for whom, how much, and how to hire you” before customers have to ask — and it shows up on Google.

How much does it cost?

Landing pages start at $400 and full websites at $700. Use the instant quote above to price your package.

How long does it take?

Within 7 days of approving the proposal, once we have your questionnaire answers and materials.

Do you work with clients outside Brazil?

Yes — that’s what this page is for. Everything happens remotely, in English, over email and video calls. You pay in US dollars through Stripe, and your site is hosted on the same infrastructure used by companies worldwide.

Do I need a domain and hosting?

You don’t need to know anything about that. We set it up for $90 — the yearly domain and hosting fees are paid by you directly to the provider (usually $15–$30 a year for a domain; hosting is free for sites like these).

What does monthly maintenance include?

For $40/month: up to 2 changes a month (text, photos, contact details, banners), small fixes, link and form checks, and support for questions. It doesn’t include new pages or a redesign — those are quoted separately. Unused requests don’t roll over.

Do you build websites for my industry?

Most likely. We’ve launched retail, consulting and home-service businesses, and the process is the same for any small business that needs to be found and understood.
